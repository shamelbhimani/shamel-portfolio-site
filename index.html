Research · Regression discontinuity · R

A Discontinuity Analysis on the Probability of Receiving Procurement Contracts During Key Electoral Cycles in the Grants-to-Contracts Conversion Pipeline

When the writ drops, a caretaker government takes over and federal procurement is meant to pause. Using organizations that had already received a federal grant, I tested whether that pause applies evenly and whether the pattern holds across the 2019 and 2025 electoral cycles.

Statistically Significant Findings from the 2025 electoral cycle:

  • −1.86%Award probability of all contracts in the 36-day post-writ window for recipients who received a grant, ~3× baseline probability
  • −2.03%Award probability of sole-source contracts specifically, ~4× baseline probability
  • +22%Award probability of contracts worth $5M or more, showing potential mishandling by the caretaker government; 5.28× baseline probability

Canada's Balance of Trade in the Face of the 2025 Tariffs

A descriptive analysis of the 2025 US tariff cycle and Canada's counter-tariffs, built on Statistics Canada merchandise trade data and WTO tariff schedules. It tracks the timeline, the applied duty burden, and which commodity sectors actually absorbed the shock.

  • 6.62%Peak applied trade-weighted tariff burden on Canadian exports
  • ~$70B USDAffected trade value

Aluminium exports were hit hardest; iron and steel declined on both sides of the border, which points to broader market conditions rather than tariffs alone. The report is a Quarto document with folded code, so every figure can be traced to the chunk that produced it.

Pipeline · Python · MySQL

Statistics Canada ETL Pipeline

An ETL pipeline that pulls time series from the StatCan Web Data Service, validates the JSON against Pydantic models, and lands it in MySQL through SQLAlchemy with a schema that widens itself as new reporting periods are released.

  • 1:1Schema mirrors the data: one table per product, one row per vector, one column per period
  • IdempotentRe-running an overlapping window never duplicates or corrupts values
  • GuardedIdentifiers are regex-validated before reaching the database; pooled connections with pre-ping

Ships with 30 vectors from the Raw Materials Price Index as a working example, but the inputs are data-driven enabling users to swap the vector list with their desired vectors allowing the program to run against any StatCan product.
